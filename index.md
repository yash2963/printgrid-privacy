# Privacy Policy — PrintGrid

**Effective date:** October 22, 2026  
**App name:** PrintGrid  
**Developer:** SayaForge  
**Contact email:** support.sayaforge@gmail.com

## 1. Introduction

PrintGrid is a photo layout and printing application that allows users to select photos from their devices, arrange multiple images on a page, customize image dimensions and positioning, choose different layouts, and prepare pages for printing.

We aim to protect your privacy by processing photos and layout settings locally on your device wherever possible. This policy explains how PrintGrid and its third-party services handle information.

## 2. Photos and Files

PrintGrid lets you select photos from your device for creating and customizing print layouts.

- Photo selection, layout arrangement, resizing, positioning, cropping, and print preparation are designed to take place on your device.
- We do not operate our own server to upload or store your selected photos.
- We do not sell your photos or use them for our own advertising.
- Photo access is limited to the permissions and functionality needed by the app.

If you choose to export, share, or print through another application, operating-system feature, or printing service, that service may process your files under its own privacy policy.

## 3. Information Handled by PrintGrid

PrintGrid's core photo layout features do not require an account. We do not intentionally collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location through these features.

The app and its integrated third-party services may process technical information needed for app functionality, advertising, security, diagnostics, and performance.

The information handled depends on the app version, permissions granted, and services enabled.

## 4. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

PrintGrid may display advertisements through Google AdMob using the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Depending on the SDK configuration, device settings, consent choices, and applicable requirements, Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as:

- IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
- Advertising IDs and other device identifiers.
- Ad impressions, interactions, and related usage information.
- Diagnostic, performance, and fraud-prevention information.
- Information used to deliver, measure, and personalize advertisements where permitted.

Google processes information under its own policies and applicable settings. We do not use your selected photos as advertising content.

For more information, please review:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Mobile Ads SDK documentation on data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure)

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not operate our own server to collect or share your photos or layout projects.

However, third-party services integrated into PrintGrid, including advertising services, may collect, process, or share certain technical, device, advertising, and usage information as described in their policies.

We do not sell your photos or personal information. Any data handling by third-party providers is subject to their applicable terms, privacy policies, and settings.

## 6. Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Photo selections and editing operations are intended to remain on your device. Files you save or export are stored according to the destination you select and the permissions available to the app.

We do not maintain a developer-operated cloud database of users' photos or layout projects.

You can manage or delete files saved by the app using the app's available features or your device's file-management tools. Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored app data, depending on Android and your backup settings.

Third-party providers may retain information they collect according to their own retention policies and applicable legal requirements.

## 7. Permissions

Depending on your device, Android version, and the features you use, PrintGrid may request access to photos, images, or files to enable photo selection, layout creation, and printing.

You can manage applicable permissions through your Android settings.

The photo layout and printing features do not require access to contacts, SMS messages, or call history.

## 8. Children's Privacy

PrintGrid is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children through our own servers.

If the app is made available to children or targets children, we will configure advertising and data-handling practices according to applicable laws and Google Play Families requirements. Advertising SDKs and settings must be appropriate for the app's declared audience.

## 9. Security

We aim to minimize personal data collection and process photos locally wherever possible.

No application or electronic system can guarantee absolute security. Information processed by third-party providers is also subject to their security practices.

## 10. Your Choices

You can manage photo and media permissions through your Android settings.

Depending on your device and region, you may also be able to reset or limit your advertising ID, manage ad personalization, and adjust relevant privacy or consent settings.

For Google's advertising settings, visit:

[Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/)

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when PrintGrid's features, integrations, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published at the privacy policy URL associated with the app. The effective date will be updated when appropriate.
